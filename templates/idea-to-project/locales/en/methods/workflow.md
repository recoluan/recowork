# Idea To Project Workflow

## Exploration and validation

Start from the [home](../workspace/index.md) to determine stage, then read the role, questions and relevant topics progressively. Clarify problem, users, constraints and success signals; compare directions and distinguish hypotheses, evidence, risks and counterevidence. Test falsifiable assumptions with minimal validation rather than more brainstorming.

Before selecting a direction or validation plan, present a direction decision package with candidates, evidence, recommendation, rejected alternatives and risks. Ask the user to choose continued exploration, assumption validation or project entry. Record restartable parked directions separately; ordinary questions need no file.

## Project entry and advancement

Only explicit confirmation of a selected direction and project entry permits complete project requirements, design, planning or implementation. Preserve direction, entry reasons, accepted risks, initial scope and success criteria. Put stable background in the brief and dynamic progress in the delivery plan; use numbered stage directories as needed.

Within authorized scope, plan, implement, verify and synchronize using the [freshness standard](./artifact-freshness-standard.md), then apply [quality gates](./quality-gates.md). Material scope or direction changes and irreversible operations still require explicit confirmation. Do not reapprove an already confirmed local task or validation plan.

## Business decisions and document maintenance

- Maintenance cannot decide business direction, change scope or declare user acceptance.
- Within an authorized task, synchronize facts, close resolved questions, repair links/indexes and perform recoverable status/navigation maintenance directly, preserving meaning and traceability; report material impact.
- Users may authorize ongoing maintenance scope and recoverable methods once. Reuse that authorization. Before out-of-scope changes, substantial rewrites changing meaning, moves, renames, physical archives or deletions, list candidates, reasons, destinations and replacements and obtain confirmation; existing explicit authorization covering those actions remains valid. Never infer deletion or irreversible permission from general tidying.
- Do not demand separate approval for each maintenance item or classify every rewrite as maintenance. If authority is unclear, complete safe synchronization/navigation first and explain the remaining concrete impact.

The [document standard](./document-standard.md) owns format and new-file criteria; the [fact map](./knowledge-structure.md) owns fact locations.
