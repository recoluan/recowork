# Artifact Freshness Standard

## Status and authority

Use `Draft`, `In review`, `Current`, `Superseded`, `Archived`. Drafts and reviews are not confirmed facts. Each topic and scope has one current default source. Reassess legacy Final labels by actual validity, not by merely changing the label.

## Fact synchronization

When a confirmed decision or validation changes a conclusion, in the same work cycle:

1. Update affected requirements, design or actions using the [fact map](./knowledge-structure.md). Preserve confirmation and reasons for important decisions; keep small decisions in the topic.
2. Match open questions by meaning, not just links. Close resolved items with a decision link; preserve unresolved parts and boundaries rather than closing everything related.
3. Update progress and next step in the delivery plan with evidence links. Change the brief only when background or goals change.
4. Compare home summaries with sources, parent navigation and related current conclusions. Mark conflicting or unconfirmed evidence as needing verification; readable prose must not conceal stale facts.

## Separate history from default reading

Mark absorbed, superseded or finished material with its status and current conclusion destination, and remove it from default navigation. Evidence still supporting current conclusions or reproduction may remain linked, with date and scope. Preserve important decisions, milestones and reproducible evidence; routine edits do not create snapshots.

History may remain in place: remove normal entries and link it in a separate “History (not current authority)” section of an existing index with replacement links. Do not read that section by default. For a separate historical entry, create an archive index on demand linking the original files. The viewer disables raw HTML; do not rely on HTML folding. Navigation can improve before moving files; do not add archive-candidate governance documents.

Only when physical archiving is needed, create `workspace/archive/index.md` and required categories. Store meaningful snapshots by category, topic and version with relative references; no empty categories. The [workflow](./workflow.md) owns permissions for moves, rewrites, deletions and batch maintenance. Hiding in-place history does not automatically exclude it from viewer search; inspect status and sources on search hits.

Check affected fact chains after material work, not the entire library every turn.
