## Stage Gate

Before project entry, ask the user to choose explicitly: continue exploring, validate a key assumption, or confirm entry for a named direction. After confirmation, record rationale, accepted risks, initial scope, and success criteria in the direction decision package, then link to `../project-brief.md`.
