# Direction Decision Package

## Conclusion First

This is the current fact record for exploration and the sole handoff package for project entry. Until confirmation, its contents are only candidate directions, assumptions, or validation results. After explicit user confirmation, record the chosen direction, accepted risks, initial scope, and success criteria, then use it as input to the project brief.

## Current Problem And Opportunity

To fill: problem, target users, scenario, constraints, success signals, and known facts.

## Candidate Directions And Evidence

To fill: each candidate's value, evidence, counterevidence, key assumptions, risks, smallest validation, and status.

## Recommendation And Unselected Directions

To fill: recommended direction and reasons for retaining, parking, excluding, or validating other directions. Copy each restartable parked direction to [Parked Ideas](../../parked-ideas.md) with its parking reason and restart condition.

## Stage-Gate Decision

- Current choice: awaiting user confirmation (continue exploring / validate a key assumption / enter project / park a direction).
- If entering project: to fill chosen direction, accepted risks, initial scope, non-goals, and success criteria.
