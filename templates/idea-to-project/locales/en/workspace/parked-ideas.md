# Parked Ideas

## Conclusion First

Record ideas or directions that are not being advanced now but may be restarted later. Parked does not mean rejected or archived: these remain scannable Current workspace items, but sit outside the active project scope and default execution plan. Each item needs a reason and restart condition. Only after an idea is explicitly abandoned, superseded, or retained solely as a historical snapshot should it enter `archive/`, after user confirmation.

### Management Rules

- When parking an idea, consolidate its key assumptions, evidence, and unresolved risks into one row or linked document; do not duplicate complete traces here.
- At each milestone review or when the user raises the direction again, check its restart condition and update Last assessed and Next step.
- When restarting it, transfer the conclusion to `direction-decision-package.md` or the relevant project document and change this table's status to Restarted.
- When it is explicitly abandoned, superseded, or needed only for traceability, propose archival first. After user confirmation, move it into `archive/` and retain a relative link.
